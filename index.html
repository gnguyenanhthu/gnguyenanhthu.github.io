Almost Lover

A Vietnamese-language lookup site for audio dramas, novels and manhua, built like an encyclopedia. It hosts no content itself. Each work links to its authors, voice actors and artists, who each get an image, a bio and a list of works.

  • Member accounts with favorites, votes and comments.
  • Five permission levels, from member to administrator.
  • Original-size image downloads from object storage.
