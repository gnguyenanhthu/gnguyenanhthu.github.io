Almost Lover

A full-stack, Vietnamese-language encyclopedia of audio dramas, novels and manhua. Members add and edit entries, moderators approve them, and every work links to its authors, voice actors and artists.

The rules live in the database, not just the interface, so a request that skips the website is held to the same permissions.

  • Five nested roles, from member to admin, enforced with PostgreSQL row-level security across more than 60 versioned migrations.
  • A submission and review workflow: contributors send drafts, moderators review them in rounds, and each moderation action is written to a log the app cannot edit.
  • Image uploads checked on the server, stored in Cloudflare R2 through presigned S3 URLs, and cleaned up by a deletion queue.
  • Novels that arrive without a cover get one generated from a template with Satori and Sharp.
  • Google sign-in, ratings, favorites, comments and weekly voting, with database types generated from the schema for type-safe queries.

A co-authored modeling study from the Mathematical Contest in Modeling (MCM) that analyzes the dynamics of sustainable tourism.

  • Modeled tourism with logistic growth and Lotka–Volterra competition equations.
  • Found equilibrium points and their stability using Jacobian matrices and eigenvalues.
  • Modeled interactions between destinations to test the effect of tourism policies.
